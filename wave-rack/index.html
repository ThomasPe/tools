Printing notes

  • Orientation: the STL is already lying on its side (profile on the bed, depth = Z). Just import and slice.
  • No supports: the part is a straight vertical extrusion, so there are no overhangs at all.
  • Adhesion: the footprint is a thin, curvy wall. Use a brim (5–8 mm) for tall prints and keep the first layer slow.
  • Strength: because every layer contains the whole profile, bending loads are carried inside the layers rather than across them. 3–4 perimeters recommended.
