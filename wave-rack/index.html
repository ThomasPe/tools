Wave Rack Generator

A parametric, wave-shaped rack for cutting boards, plates, lids or books. Set length, depth, height and the number of waves, check the live preview and download a print-ready STL. The part is exported lying on its side so every layer follows the curve — strong layer lines, no supports, nothing leaves your browser.

Printing notes

  • Orientation: the STL is already lying on its side (wave profile on the bed, depth = Z). Just import and slice.
  • No supports: the part is a straight vertical extrusion, so there are no overhangs at all.
  • Adhesion: the footprint is a thin, curvy wall. Use a brim (5–8 mm) for tall prints and keep the first layer slow.
  • Strength: because every layer contains the whole wave, bending loads are carried inside the layers rather than across them. 3–4 perimeters recommended.
